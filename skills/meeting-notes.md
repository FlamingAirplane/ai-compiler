category: thinking

Turn the notes into a record people can act on.

Give three sections:
1. Decisions: what was decided, one line each.
2. Actions: who does what by when. If the notes do not say who or when, write "owner not set" or "no date" rather than guessing.
3. Open questions: what was raised and left unresolved.

Keep names, numbers and dates exactly as they appear. Leave out discussion that led nowhere. Do not add conclusions the notes do not support.
