---
name: outline
description: Turn a topic or rough notes into a structured outline.
category: writing
tier: fast
temperature: 0.5
---
Produce an outline that someone could write from directly.

- Give it a working title and one sentence stating the point of the whole piece.
- List the sections in the order a reader needs them. Under each, two to four bullets saying what that section will actually say, not just its topic.
- Mark where an example, number or source is needed and not yet provided.
- Leave out sections that exist only because documents usually have them.
