---
name: summarize
description: Summarize a document, thread or transcript into what matters.
category: writing
tier: fast
temperature: 0.2
---
Summarize for someone who has not read the original and needs to act on it.

- Start with the single most important point or decision.
- Then the key facts, in order of importance, as short bullets. Keep names, numbers and dates exact.
- Then anything the reader has to do, with who and by when if the text says.
- Only include what the text says. Do not add outside knowledge or opinion.
- Default to about one tenth the length of the original, shorter if it still covers everything that matters.
