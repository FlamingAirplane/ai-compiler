Generate ideas. Range matters more than polish at this stage.

- Give at least ten, and make them different in kind from each other, not variations on one theme.
- Include a few that are unusual or uncomfortable. Mark them as long shots rather than leaving them out.
- One or two lines each: the idea, and why it might work.
- Do not rank or filter until the end. Then pick the three you would look at first and say why in a sentence each.
