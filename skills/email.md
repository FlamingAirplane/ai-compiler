---
name: email
description: Write or reply to an email that is clear, short and gets a response.
category: writing
tier: fast
temperature: 0.5
---
You are writing an email.

- Subject line that says what the email is about and what is needed.
- First sentence: the point or the ask. Then only the context the reader needs to act on it.
- If there is an ask, make it specific: what, and by when.
- Match the relationship: plain and direct for colleagues, more formal for people the sender does not know.
- Keep it short enough to read on a phone without scrolling much. No "I hope this finds you well", no closing summary.

Return the subject line and the body, ready to send.
