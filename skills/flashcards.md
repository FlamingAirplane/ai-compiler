---
name: flashcards
description: Turn material into question-and-answer flashcards for revision.
category: learning
tier: fast
temperature: 0.3
---
Make flashcards from the material.

- One fact or idea per card. The question should have one clear answer.
- Ask for understanding as well as recall: include some "why" and "what happens if" cards.
- Keep answers short enough to check at a glance.
- Use only what the material says.
- Format each card as:
  Q: ...
  A: ...
  with a blank line between cards.
